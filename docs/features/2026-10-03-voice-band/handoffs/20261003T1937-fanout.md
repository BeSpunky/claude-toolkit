State contract (U1 writes, U2 reads), all under ~/.claude/bespunky-voice/:
- `.speaking.pid` exists ⇔ an utterance is playing (removed when it ends or is stopped);
  `last-utterance.txt` = its text (mtime = when it started).
- `.listening.pid` exists ⇔ a recording is open; `.hearing` = transcript so far
  (both removed when listen.sh exits).
- `scripts/voice.sh stop` silences speech AND ends any open recording; `voice.sh replay`.
