## Design decisions (taken; see commits for the code)
- **A `node` layer is added** (`layers/node.ts`, evidence `package.json`). contracts/layers.md said "add it when a phase
  gives it a generator or a devcontainer fragment" — phase 2 does: it owns the typescript-node image, the
  node_modules volume, `node_modules/.bin` on PATH, CHOKIDAR polling, eslint/prettier/TS editor bits and the
  package-manager install. Without package.json (the wrapper host) the container is `devcontainers/base:debian`
  + Node as a feature (the Nx floor and house tooling need Node at runtime).
- **`.nx` volume belongs to the node host, not `nx`**: on the wrapper host `.nx/` carries the COMMITTED
  `nxw.js`; a volume over `.nx` would hide it and break `./nx` inside the container.
- Fragments are DATA on `descriptor.devcontainer`; the devcontainer generator composes the active layers'
  fragments (+ the voice intent's fragment) into devcontainer.json (comments carried as `why`) and ONE composed
  post-create.sh (pieces ordered by phase). Composed script rather than post-create.d/: keeps the provenance
  marker / beside-path / chain-feature machinery and the `bash -n` proof at generation time, and nothing stale
  to garbage-collect when a layer leaves.
- One plugin source: `descriptor.claudePlugins` → both claude-settings `enabledPlugins` and the post-create
  pre-install list. browser-automation moves to `web` (its Chromium is web-only now).
