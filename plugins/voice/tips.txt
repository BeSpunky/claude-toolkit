# Spinner tips for this plugin, shown now and then while Claude works (see plugins/bespunky/hooks/tips.mjs).
# One tip per line; blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored. Keep each short enough to glance at.

Stepping away from the keyboard? /bespunky-voice:voice auto on reads Claude's questions aloud
Say "let's do this hands-free" to answer Claude's questions by speaking
/bespunky-voice:voice test checks your speech-to-text and text-to-speech setup in one go
Missed what the voice said? /bespunky-voice:voice replay says it again — or just say "repeat that" when it's listening
Voice talking too long? Start typing — it stops the moment you send. Or say "never mind" while it listens
