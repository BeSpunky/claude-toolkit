# Spinner tips for this plugin, shown now and then while Claude works (see plugins/bespunky/hooks/tips.mjs).
# One tip per line; blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored. Keep each short enough to glance at.

Stepping away from the keyboard? /speak auto on reads Claude's questions aloud
Say "let's do this hands-free" to answer Claude's questions by speaking
/speak test checks your speech-to-text and text-to-speech setup in one go
Missed what the voice said? /speak replay says it again — or just say "repeat that" when it's listening
Voice talking too long? Start typing — it stops the moment you send. Or say "never mind" while it listens
While the voice talks or listens, the band above the prompt shows it live — press r to replay, s to stop
Voice sounding robotic? /speak status names the engine that will speak, and why — the band warns you too
No voice engine yet? Just ask to talk by voice — Claude installs what's missing itself
