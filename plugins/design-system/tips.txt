Reaching for a hex colour or magic px? design-system-first turns it into a token instead
A UI pattern used twice? Promote it into the design system once (house Angular: nx g @bespunky/nx-tools:ds-component <name>)
Adding dark mode? design-tokens-and-theming rebinds tokens per mode, no swapped stylesheets, no flash
