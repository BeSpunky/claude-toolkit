What's faked (and why)

    Purple pins are the design intent — hover or focus one to see what a low-fidelity thing will become. Open a concept, press c, and click the exact spot (or move the crosshair with the arrow keys and press Enter) to leave an orange comment right where you point.

    Deciding: when one concept wins, press Choose this concept to lock the direction to build — or None of these to send them all back to be reconceived.
